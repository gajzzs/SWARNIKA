# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript, chosen by the user. Styling: plain CSS modules / global CSS (delegated). No backend yet; forms and appointment requests are client-side demos ready to wire to an API.

## Users

- **Primary:** Indian brides-to-be and their families (often the mother or the bride herself) planning a wedding trousseau 3–9 months ahead. They research online on a phone, compare price and purity, then visit or video-call before buying a high-ticket piece.
- **Secondary:** festive and gifting buyers (Diwali, Akshaya Tritiya, anniversaries) looking for an heirloom-grade piece rather than fashion jewellery.

## Product Purpose

The website is the jeweller's digital showroom: it presents collections with real photography and transparent prices, earns trust about purity and making, and converts interest into a showroom appointment, video consultation, or WhatsApp enquiry. Success = qualified appointments and enquiries, not cart checkout (no e-commerce checkout in v1).

## Positioning

*(Assumed, invented by Claude at the user's request — replace with real facts.)* **Swarnika** — a family atelier of hand-set kundan, polki and jadau jewellery from Jaipur. Its claim: every piece is made by named karigars in its own workshop, and every price is shown openly with a gold/stone/making/GST breakup — heirloom craft with nothing hidden.

## Operating Context

- Indian fine-jewellery buying rituals: gold-rate checking, BIS hallmark (HUID) verification, price breakup (metal value + stones + making charges + 3% GST), old-gold exchange, buyback, trousseau planning, muhurat dates.
- Prices in ₹ with Indian digit grouping (₹2,89,500). Gold priced per gram by karat (22K/18K) and changes daily.
- Contact channels Indians actually use: WhatsApp first, phone, showroom visit, video call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- v1 pages: Home, Collections (filterable catalogue), Product detail (with price breakup), About (story/atelier), Contact (appointment + showroom).
- Researched features to include: today's gold rate strip, transparent price breakup, BIS hallmark/HUID & certification badges, book appointment / video consultation, WhatsApp enquiry, wishlist, trousseau/bridal planner, filters (category, metal, occasion, price), size guide, old-gold exchange & lifetime buyback info.
- **Undecided / placeholder:** real brand name, address, phone, gold rates, product inventory, prices and certifications. All shown values are demonstration data and must be replaced.
- Virtual AR try-on: out of scope for v1 (needs a vendor SDK); may be teased later.

## Brand Commitments

- Classy, luxurious, with a personality rooted in Indian jewellery (user brief).
- Palette and typography delegated to Claude.

## Evidence on Hand

- Imagery: openly licensed photographs (CC0 museum collections via Wikimedia Commons; CC BY / CC BY-SA photographs) of real Indian jewellery — kundan, jadau, temple, jhumka, bangles, bridal portraits. They are stand-ins for the jeweller's own product photography and must be credited per licence (credits page/footer).
- No real testimonials, press, awards, customer counts, or founding date exist. Do not fabricate them as facts; any such content is labelled as sample.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before temptation: purity, price breakup and hallmark are first-class, never fine print.
2. Craft is the hero: show the hand and the workshop behind each piece.
3. Conversation over checkout: every page leads to a human (appointment, video call, WhatsApp).
4. Mobile-first research: the bride's family browses on phones; every key action works one-handed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Text contrast on gold/dark grounds verified; reduced-motion respected; prices readable by screen readers (₹ spoken correctly).
