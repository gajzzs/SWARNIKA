# Surface brief: Swarnika marketing site (home, collections, product, about, contact)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: brides-to-be and families planning a trousseau; mobile-first research leading to a showroom or video appointment. Action: book a sitting (in-store or video) or enquire on WhatsApp. Proof: transparent price breakup, BIS/HUID seals, named karigars (sample), consistent AI-generated concept photography (labelled synthetic). Constraints: prices, rates, names are sample data; no fabricated testimonials or press.

USER REDESIGN v3 (2026-11-12): the dark maison read "a bit dark"; the user asked for lighter colour, a site that "feels alive" (not pulse indicators, not clutter), custom scrollbars, a better product rail, and pinned five reference sites: light editorial jewellery houses with worn-on-model photography, a large serif with italic accents, an arch-framed hero portrait with a gold circular CTA, floating product card, [ bracket ] category links, a blurred warm services band, occasion and category image tiles, FAQ. Models to use: google/gemini-3.1-flash-image (stills), recraft/recraft-v4.1-vector (ornaments), google/gemini-omni-1.2-flash (film). This supersedes the dark maison.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The ivory salon in daylight: a Jaipur sitting room at mid-morning where the bride, the tray and the price are all seen in the same honest light. Alive means light and breath, never chrome: a portrait that breathes inside a jharokha arch, a seal that turns slowly, a blurred film of gold behind the services, ornaments that draw themselves once.
OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory #F7F2EA, sand band #EFE7DA, stone wells #E7DCCB, paper #FCFAF6; ink #241B14 for type; antique gold #B38B4D for hairlines, fills and the seal, gold-ink #7D5A20 for italic accents and small gold text; one deep kattha-brown #2A1A15 for the rate strip, the living services band and the footer. Bodoni Moda display with italic accent words, Jost text (Mukta for Devanagari), tabular numerals. Pill buttons in ink. Pointed jharokha arch used once, in the hero.
STORY: The visitor meets a bride lit by a window, reads that nothing is hidden, sees the pieces worn and then laid on linen, turns a price over, learns how the house sits with families, and books a sitting or WhatsApps.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, Bodoni headline "Gold deserves the whole truth." with "whole" in gold italic, lede, Book a sitting (ink pill) + WhatsApp (line pill), video-call note, [ category ] links on a hairline. Right, the living bride portrait in a pointed arch with a gold hairline arch drawn around it, a rotating gold seal CTA on its edge, and a floating paper card for the necklace she wears.
FORM: User-pinned references (five light editorial jewellery houses) applied to Swarnika's Jaipur kundan world, superseding the dark maison and seed:c1aca1942e8e2a90.
